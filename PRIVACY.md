# BejView privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-14. This policy covers BejView on every platform: the Microsoft Store version, the WinGet and zip versions for Windows, and the Linux version.

## The short version

BejView collects nothing, sends nothing and connects to nothing. Everything it does happens on your own computer.

## What BejView does with your data

- **No personal information.** BejView doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.
- **No network access.** BejView makes no network connections. It has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, accounts or automatic updates. (The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's own privacy policy.)
- **Your files stay where they are.** BejView opens only the files you choose, and only to read them. It never changes, uploads or sends them anywhere. It writes a new file only when you ask it to (Save selection), to a place you pick.
- **What it keeps on your computer.** BejView saves its settings (such as dark mode, text size, highlight rules and your list of recently opened files) and a troubleshooting log in its own folder on your computer: `%APPDATA%\BejView` on Windows (the Microsoft Store version uses its own private copy of that folder, which uninstalling removes), and `~/.config/BejView` on Linux. The log records file paths, sizes and what BejView did, never the contents of your files. `bejview paths` shows exactly where these files are. You can delete them at any time.
- **Help menu link.** If you choose Help > Read Me (opens GitHub), BejView opens the project's web page in your browser. That is your browser visiting GitHub, under GitHub's privacy policy; BejView itself sends nothing.

## Children

BejView collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. The project's history on GitHub shows every earlier version.
